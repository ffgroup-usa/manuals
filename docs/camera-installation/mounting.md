## Camera Mounting Angle

The camera mounting angle should **not exceed 45°**.

- Ideal angle: **below 30°**
- Maximum supported angle: **45°**

Excessive tilt can negatively impact recognition accuracy.

---

## Horizontal License Plate Alignment

:::warning
License plates should not be tilted more than **5° horizontally** in the image.
:::

Ensure that license plates appear horizontally aligned in the live view. If the plate appears tilted beyond this threshold, adjust the camera position accordingly.

---

## Minimum License Plate Size

Recommended minimum plate widths:

| Plate Type | Minimum Width | Optimal Width |
|----------|--------------|---------------|
| EU (single row) | 100 px | 130 px |
| USA | 80 px | 100 px |
| Two-row plates | 80 px | 100 px |
| AU / NZ | 100 px | 100 px |

Use the camera’s pixel counter during installation to verify plate size.
