Proper camera installation is critical for achieving optimal recognition accuracy with CAMMRA AI.

This section describes recommended mounting angles, license plate alignment, power handling, and storage configuration. Following these guidelines ensures reliable operation and consistent recognition results under varying traffic and lighting conditions.

CAMMRA AI is designed to operate in demanding traffic environments, including multi-lane roads and high-speed scenarios. Correct physical installation is the foundation for successful deployment.
